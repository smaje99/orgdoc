# Brand voice for OrgDoc documents

Read only the profile selected by the operation. Use its `voice` object as editorial guidance; keep it separate from `visual` tokens and document business rules.

## Profile fields

- `language`: preferred output language. OrgDoc examples use Spanish.
- `tone`: the brand's default manner, such as clear, professional, direct, didactic, approachable, or respectful.
- `avoid`: habits or wording the brand wants to avoid. Apply these preferences only when they do not remove necessary technical or factual meaning.
- `approved_examples`: optional passages approved by the organization. Learn their register, sentence length, and terminology. Do not reuse their facts or wording as evidence for a new document.

## Selection and conflicts

Resolve the operation first, then use the brand it selects. Do not combine examples or preferences from another brand in the same document. A user can specify audience, purpose, and required terminology; preserve those requirements while applying the selected brand's voice. Ask when those instructions conflict in a way that changes meaning.

## Field-level guidance

Brand voice applies to editable narrative prose. It does not authorize edits to invoice values, IDs, dates, amounts, rates, source references, quotations, citations, or other fields declared protected by the selected document type. For invoice line descriptions, make only the requested wording change and retain the described service or product.
